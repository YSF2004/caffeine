1980

The Dream Begins

In the bustling streets of Marrakesh, a young barista dreams of opening a coffee shop that fuses traditional Moroccan flavors with modern coffee culture. Inspired by the rich history of coffee in the region, the idea of "Caffeine" starts brewing.

1990

The First Beans

After years of training under expert roasters, the founder of Caffeine sources the finest coffee beans from around the world, including Moroccan-grown beans from the Atlas Mountains. The search for the perfect blend begins.

2000

Opening Day

Caffeine officially opens its doors in the heart of Marrakesh. The shop combines the essence of Moroccan hospitality with specialty coffee drinks. Locals and tourists alike gather to enjoy unique coffee creations like Moroccan-spiced lattes and traditional mint tea with a twist.

2005

The Roastery is Born

To ensure the highest quality coffee, Caffeine expands and creates its own roastery. The shop begins experimenting with different roasting techniques, pushing the boundaries of flavor profiles while still maintaining traditional Moroccan tastes.

2007

Community and Culture

Caffeine becomes more than just a coffee shop; it evolves into a cultural hub. Art exhibits, live music performances, and poetry readings are hosted regularly, fostering a vibrant community around coffee culture in Marrakesh.

2010

International Recognition

Word spreads beyond Marrakesh as Caffeine receives recognition at international coffee festivals. Tourists from all over the world stop by to experience the unique fusion of Moroccan coffee culture and modern brewing techniques.

2025

The Future of Caffeine

With plans to expand globally, Caffeine continues to innovate and stay ahead of trends in the coffee industry. The Marrakesh location remains a local favorite, offering a warm haven for anyone in search of a perfect cup of coffee and an unforgettable experience.
